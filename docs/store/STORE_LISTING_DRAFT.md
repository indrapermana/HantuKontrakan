## Indonesian copy

Short description:

> Kelola kontrakan para hantu, jaga kebahagiaan mereka, dan atasi kejadian mistis.

Full description:

> Selamat datang di Hantu Kontrakan, game pengelolaan santai tempat Pocong, Kuntilanak, Tuyul, dan Genderuwo menjadi penghuni kontrakanmu. Tempatkan penghuni, tingkatkan kamar, pasang furnitur yang cocok, kumpulkan Koin Arwah, dan selesaikan keluhan unik mereka.
>
> Mainkan progres inti secara offline. Iklan berhadiah bersifat opsional dan hanya memberi bonus saat kamu memilih untuk menontonnya.

## English copy

Short description:

> Manage a ghostly boarding house, keep tenants happy, and solve spooky incidents.

Full description:

> Welcome to Hantu Kontrakan, a cozy management game where Pocong, Kuntilanak, Tuyul, and Genderuwo become your supernatural tenants. Place residents, upgrade rooms, choose suitable furniture, collect Spirit Coins, and resolve their unusual complaints.
>
> Core progress is playable offline. Rewarded ads are optional and grant a bonus only when you choose to watch one.
