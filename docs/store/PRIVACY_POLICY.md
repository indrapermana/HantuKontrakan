# Hantu Kontrakan Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 October 2026

Hantu Kontrakan is provided by PT Indesya Karya Digital ("Indesya Karya Digital", "we", "us", or "our"). This policy explains what information the game stores or processes and the choices available to players.

## Data stored on the device

Hantu Kontrakan stores game progress, economy state, settings, language selection, and consent choices locally on the player's device. The game does not require an account and does not currently synchronize saves to a cloud service.

Local data remains on the device until the player resets the game, clears the app's storage, or uninstalls the app. Those actions delete the corresponding local data. Because there is no player account or cloud save, we cannot remotely retrieve or delete this local data.

## Optional analytics and diagnostics

Anonymous gameplay analytics and external crash or error reporting are separate optional settings and are disabled by default. No analytics or crash-reporting provider is configured in the current release candidate. Enabling either setting does not change offline gameplay. If we add a provider later, we will update this policy and the applicable store disclosures before releasing that change.

## Advertising and consent

The game contains optional rewarded advertising through Google Mobile Ads and uses Google's User Messaging Platform for applicable consent choices. A player chooses whether to watch a rewarded ad; the core offline game remains playable without watching one.

Google and its advertising partners may process device identifiers, advertising identifiers, consent information, IP address, approximate location inferred from network information, ad interactions, and diagnostic information under their own policies. The exact processing depends on the player's region, consent choices, device settings, and the advertising services available at the time.

Development and test builds use Google-provided test advertising identifiers. Production advertising identifiers will be configured before advertising is enabled in a distributed release.

## Data sharing and sale

We do not sell player data. Information processed by Google advertising services may be shared with Google and its advertising partners for ad delivery, consent, fraud prevention, measurement, and related purposes described in Google's policies.

## Security

We use the protections provided by the operating system and the transport security of configured service providers. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## Children's privacy

Hantu Kontrakan is not currently configured as a child-directed app. Store target-audience settings and advertising treatment must be reviewed before distribution. We do not knowingly collect account information from children because the game has no account system.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when game features, service providers, or legal requirements change. The published policy will show its current effective date.
